## Objective

Identify a specific information gap, retrieve suitable historical or knowledge-base evidence, and stop with explicit uncertainty when context remains insufficient.

## Research questions

- How can implicit references become grounded search requests?
- How do adaptive and multi-hop retrieval decide the next evidence need and a stopping condition?
- How do temporal validity, document versions, scope restrictions, cost, and abstention affect retrieval?
